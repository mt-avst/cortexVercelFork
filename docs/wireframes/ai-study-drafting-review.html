What this adds, and what it refuses to do

One brief replaces the first three steps of typing

Was: type, delivery, title, purpose, description, participant type, duration and every question keyed by hand before Review can say anything.

Every value carries its provenance

Why: a drafted form that looks finished gets published unread. From brief, assumed and gap are three different levels of trust, shown as three different marks.

Consent comes from the template, never the model

Why: the server already resolves wording against approved templates and downgrades anything else to custom. The drafter is not allowed to write it at all.

Status is Draft and publish stays in the form

Why: Apply only fills fields. Save and Publish are the same clicks as today, through the same publish gate.

Links are copied, never composed

Why: an invented URL is the one hallucination that reaches a participant. A link the brief does not contain is a gap, not a guess.

Dates and session slots are gaps in v1

Why: "next week" is relative to a clock the model does not have. Slots stay on Session Management, exactly where they are today.
