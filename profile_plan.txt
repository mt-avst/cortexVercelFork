AdaptaLabs Profiles & AdaptaBits
==================================

Core Idea:
Turn internal product testing and research participation into an ongoing, light-hearted competition where participation earns points, prizes, and prestige.

------------------------------------------------------------

Core Mechanics:
- Profiles as Player Cards:
  Each employee has a personal profile showing points, achievements, and activity history.

- Points for Participation:
  Earn points for joining research sessions, completing surveys, and testing products.

- Leaderboards:
  Live monthly leaderboard for short-term motivation, cumulative annual leaderboard for the grand final.

- Monthly Prizes:
  Top scorers receive swag, vouchers, or recognition.

- Grand Final:
  Annual top scorers enter a final challenge or draw to win the AdaptaLabs Champion title.

- Visual Tone:
  Playful “science lab meets arcade” design – badges, sparks, levelling meters, bright highlights.

------------------------------------------------------------

Design Goals:
- Encourage ongoing engagement with testing and research activities.
- Make participation fun, social, and culturally rewarding.
- Use visual feedback and competition to make contribution feel meaningful.

------------------------------------------------------------

Possible UI Elements:
- Progress bar or level meter on profile.
- Badge grid for earned achievements.
- Leaderboard with monthly and annual tabs.
- Animated confetti or spark effect when earning points.
- Champion banner for top contributors.
