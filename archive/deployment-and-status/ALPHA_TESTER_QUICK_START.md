# Quick Start Guide for Alpha Testers

**Welcome to AdaptaLabs Alpha Testing!**

This quick guide will help you get started testing the application.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started (5 minutes)

### 1. Access the Application
- **URL**: https://adapta-labs-p62q.vercel.app
- No special setup required - just open in your browser

### 2. Sign In Options

**Option A: Google SSO (Recommended)**
- Click "Sign in with Google"
- Use your company Google account

**Option B: Demo Login (For Testing)**
- Click "Demo Login" on the landing page
- Creates a temporary demo account

### 3. Try These First

1. **Browse Opportunities** (No login needed)
   - View the list of opportunities
   - Click on any opportunity to see details
   - Try filtering by type

2. **Book a Session** (Login required)
   - Sign in
   - Find an opportunity with available sessions
   - Click "Book" on a session
   - Check your email for confirmation

3. **Manage Your Bookings**
   - Click "My Bookings" in the header
   - Try canceling a booking
   - Try rescheduling a booking

## 🐛 Reporting Issues

### How to Report

1. **Use the Feedback Form** (Easiest)
   - Click your profile icon in header
   - Click "Send Feedback"
   - Fill out the form

2. **Service Desk**
   - https://adaptavistlabs.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/80

3. **Email**
   - nfine@adaptavist.com

### What to Include

When reporting issues:
- **What you were trying to do**
- **What happened** (or didn't happen)
- **Any error messages**
- **Browser and device** you're using
- **Screenshots** (if helpful)
